Overview

What Aximove Does

Aximove turns any camera into a movement coach. Here's everything working under the hood.

Feature 01

Automatic Pose Analysis

Every frame of your video runs through a pose-estimation model that tracks 33 body landmarks. There are no wearables or sensors — just your camera. Aximove converts the movement into a precise stream of joint angles and body positions ready for scoring.

Feature 02

Time-Aligned Scoring

Your attempt is aligned to the reference with Dynamic Time Warping, so moving faster or slower never costs you points — only your form does. The result is split into a Technique score (joint angles) and a Posture score (body positioning), blended into one clear overall percentage.

Feature 03

Per-Joint Feedback & AI Coaching

Aximove pinpoints exactly which joints drifted from the reference and by how much. An AI coach then turns those raw numbers into plain-language fixes, cues, and accessory drills tailored to your specific exercise — so you know not just your score, but how to beat it next time.

Feature 04

Live Skeleton & Reference Overlay

Record with a real-time skeleton drawn over you, plus a ghost of the reference pose to line up against before you begin. Prefer something you already filmed? Upload a clip and score it just the same.

Feature 05

Community Gallery

Browse reference movements shared by the community, upvote the best, and leave comments. Hit "Use This" on any video to instantly score yourself against it — and see how many others have trained with it too.

Feature 06

Replay & Track Progress

Every session is saved to your dashboard with a skeleton-overlaid replay and full feedback. Rewatch your form, compare attempts, and watch your scores climb over time.
